# Economics Projects

This page provides a curated directory of concrete, actionable project walkthroughs, step-by-step methodological guides, open templates, and real-world analytical workflows for self-directed learners who have completed the Economics Core Curriculum and/or Advanced Topics and want to apply their knowledge through tangible projects.

- **Conduct a Data Analysis Project** — Pull, clean, analyze, and interpret economic data from public sources like FRED and the World Bank.
- **Write a Policy Brief** — Translate economic analysis into concise, actionable policy recommendations for decision-makers.
- **Write an Economics Research Paper** — Design, execute, and write up a small empirical research study using public data.
- **Replicate an Econometric Study** — Reproduce the results of a published economics paper and potentially extend the analysis.
- **Build an Economic Data Visualization or Dashboard** — Create interactive visualizations and dashboards of economic indicators.
- **Run a Cost-Benefit or Economic Impact Analysis** — Evaluate the economic effects of policies, projects, or events using established frameworks.

## Conduct a Data Analysis Project

Projects in this category involve pulling economic data from public APIs, cleaning it, running analysis, and interpreting the results. These guides show you the complete workflow from data acquisition to insight.

- [Exploring International Debt Statistics Using the World Bank API (World Bank)](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/opendata/exploring-international-debt-statistics-using-world-bank-api-python-and-r) - a step-by-step guide in both Python and R showing how to retrieve, explore, and visualize data from the World Bank's International Debt Statistics database using their public API.

- [FRED API in Python: US Federal Reserve Data Analysis (Medium)](https://medium.com/datons/download-and-analyze-fred-data-automatically-with-python-5d1bf362c645) - a step-by-step tutorial on automatically downloading, exporting, and visualizing economic data from the St. Louis Fed using the FRED API with Python.

- [DIME R Training (World Bank)](https://github.com/worldbank/dime-r-training) - a complete R training course from the World Bank's impact evaluation department, designed for development research and covering common data analysis tasks from basic R syntax through advanced analysis workflows.

- [fredapi: Python API for FRED (GitHub)](https://github.com/mortada/fredapi) - the most widely used Python wrapper for the FRED API, with documentation and examples showing how to pull Federal Reserve economic data into pandas DataFrames for analysis.

## Write a Policy Brief

These guides walk you through translating economic research and data analysis into a concise, persuasive document aimed at policymakers or other non-technical audiences.

- [Policy Briefs (UNC Writing Center)](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/policy-briefs) - a comprehensive guide from the University of North Carolina Writing Center explaining what policy briefs are, how they differ from other writing assignments, and how to structure one effectively.

- [How to Write a Policy Brief (IDRC)](https://idrc-crdi.ca/en/funding/resources-idrc-grantees/how-write-policy-brief) - a practical guide from the International Development Research Centre covering structure, audience, language, and formatting for effective policy briefs.

- [Policy Briefs 101: A Guide for Translating Research Findings into Policy Impact (California Policy Lab)](https://www.capolicylab.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Policy-Brief-Guide.pdf) - a PDF guide developed for UCLA researchers covering how to convert academic writing into policy brief format, with templates and examples.

## Write an Economics Research Paper

These guides help you design and execute a small empirical research project, from formulating a question through data analysis to writing up the results in standard economics paper format.

- [Writing Economics: A Guide for Harvard Economics Concentrators (Harvard Writing Project)](https://writingproject.fas.harvard.edu/file_url/252) - a comprehensive guide on how economists approach research and writing, covering research questions, empirical methods, and paper structure.

- [Writing Tips for Economics Research Papers (IZA Discussion Paper)](https://docs.iza.org/dp15057.pdf) - a detailed guide by Plamen Nikolov covering the three pillars of economics writing: research, organization, and analysis, with specific advice on structure, argumentation, and empirical presentation.

- [A Guide for Student-led Undergraduate Research in Empirical Microeconomics (Journal of Economics Teaching)](http://downloads.journalofeconomicsteaching.org/5/3/3-2.pdf) - a step-by-step guide following the full arc of an empirical economics research project, from preparation through writing, presenting, and publication.

## Replicate an Econometric Study

These resources guide you through reproducing the results of a published economics paper, a project type widely regarded as one of the best ways to learn applied econometrics.

- [Development Research in Practice: The DIME Analytics Data Handbook (World Bank)](https://worldbank.github.io/dime-data-handbook) - the definitive free handbook on reproducible research workflows, covering everything from data acquisition and cleaning through analysis and documentation, designed for empirical researchers working with development data.

- [A Guide for Student-led Undergraduate Research in Empirical Microeconomics (Journal of Economics Teaching)](http://downloads.journalofeconomicsteaching.org/5/3/3-2.pdf) - covers the "replicate and extend" approach, where students reproduce a published paper's results and then extend the analysis in a new direction.

## Build an Economic Data Visualization or Dashboard

These guides show you how to create interactive visualizations and dashboards for economic data, ranging from simple charts to full web applications.

- [Exploring International Debt Statistics Using the World Bank API (World Bank)](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/opendata/exploring-international-debt-statistics-using-world-bank-api-python-and-r) - includes complete code for creating interactive line graphs and visualizations of international debt data in both Python and R.

- [Mastering Shiny (Hadley Wickham)](https://mastering-shiny.org) - the definitive free online book on building interactive web applications in R using the Shiny framework, covering everything from your first app through advanced dashboard development.

- [Economic Data Analysis Project with Python Pandas (Kaggle/YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R67XuYc9NQ4) - a complete video walkthrough showing how to use the FRED API with Python to pull economic indicators, join them together, and create visualizations using Plotly.

## Run a Cost-Benefit or Economic Impact Analysis

These guides and templates walk you through evaluating the economic effects of policies, projects, or events, whether for policy analysis or program evaluation.

- [Economic Impact Assessment Guideline (Queensland Government)](https://www.statedevelopment.qld.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0012/33420/economic-impact-assessment-guideline.pdf) - a comprehensive government guideline covering the methodology, data requirements, and reporting standards for conducting economic impact assessments.

- [BEA Updates Tool for Economic Impact Studies (US Bureau of Economic Analysis)](https://www.bea.gov/news/blog/2021-05-06-bea-updates-tool-economic-impact-studies) - an introduction to the BEA's RIMS II (Regional Input-Output Modeling System), a free tool for assessing the economic impacts of projects and events on local and regional economies.
