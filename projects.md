## Build your own economic data pipelines

- [Exploring International Debt Statistics Using the World Bank API in Python and R (World Bank)](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/opendata/exploring-international-debt-statistics-using-world-bank-api-python-and-r) - A step-by-step code walkthrough demonstrating how to programmatically query the World Bank API, clean indicator tables, and export cross-country debt statistics.
- [Economic Data Analysis Project with Python Pandas (Rob Mulla)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R67XuYc9NQ4) - A complete video tutorial detailing how to fetch macroeconomic series using the FRED API, clean and merge time-series data, and structure indicators for analysis.
- [fredapi: Python API for FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis)](https://github.com/mortada/fredapi) - A production-ready Python client with documentation and examples for pulling point-in-time vintage data from ALFRED and assembling tidy pandas dataframes.
- [DIME Analytics R Training (World Bank)](https://github.com/worldbank/dime-r-training) - A hands-on training repository from the World Bank Development Impact Evaluation group covering reproducible data cleaning, wrangling, and pipeline construction in R.

## Build your own interactive economic dashboards

- [Mastering Shiny (Hadley Wickham)](https://mastering-shiny.org) - The definitive web guide to developing reactive, interactive data dashboards and web applications using R.
- [Get Started with Streamlit (Streamlit Docs)](https://docs.streamlit.io/get-started) - Official tutorial on building and deploying data-driven web applications and filterable analytical dashboards in Python.
- [Economic Data Analysis Project with Python Pandas (Rob Mulla)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R67XuYc9NQ4) - Video walkthrough building an interactive macroeconomic indicator dashboard with Plotly Express and pandas.

## Conduct your own replication studies

- [Guide for Accelerating Computational Reproducibility in the Social Sciences (BITSS / ACRe)](https://bitss.github.io/ACRE/) - A comprehensive handbook developed with the American Economic Association Data Editor outlining step-by-step protocols for assessing, reproducing, and conducting robustness checks on published economics papers.
- [Development Research in Practice: The DIME Analytics Data Handbook (World Bank)](https://worldbank.github.io/dime-data-handbook) - An open-source operational handbook detailing best practices for code structure, data provenance, and reproducible research workflows in empirical economics.
- [A Guide for Student-led Undergraduate Research in Empirical Microeconomics (Journal of Economics Teaching)](http://downloads.journalofeconomicsteaching.org/5/3/3-2.pdf) - A practical methodological guide walking through the "replicate and extend" model, where learners reproduce a published empirical paper and test novel variations.

## Write your own empirical research papers

- [Writing Economics: A Guide for Harvard Economics Concentrators (Harvard Writing Project)](https://writingproject.fas.harvard.edu/files/hwp/files/writingeconomics.pdf) - A foundational guide explaining how economists structure empirical papers, formulate testable research hypotheses, document data limitations, and present regression findings.
- [Writing Tips for Economics Research Papers (Plamen Nikolov / IZA)](https://docs.iza.org/dp15057.pdf) - A detailed technical guide covering empirical paper architecture, model specification descriptions, table layouts, and identification arguments.
- [A Guide for Student-led Undergraduate Research in Empirical Microeconomics (Journal of Economics Teaching)](http://downloads.journalofeconomicsteaching.org/5/3/3-2.pdf) - Follows the lifecycle of an independent empirical paper from identifying data sources to drafting methodology and addressing referee feedback.

## Write your own policy briefs

- [Policy Briefs (UNC Writing Center)](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/policy-briefs) - A foundational guide to the policy brief genre, detailing audience analysis, executive summary framing, and actionable policy recommendation structures.
- [How to Write a Policy Brief (IDRC - International Development Research Centre)](https://idrc-crdi.ca/en/funding/resources-idrc-grantees/how-write-policy-brief) - Practical instructions on translating complex empirical evidence into high-impact, two-page policy memos for non-specialist decision-makers.
- [Policy Briefs 101: A Guide for Translating Research Findings into Policy Impact (California Policy Lab)](https://www.capolicylab.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Policy-Brief-Guide.pdf) - A practitioner manual developed for researchers with outlines, graphic design considerations, and editing timelines for policy translations.

## Conduct your own cost-benefit analyses

- [OMB Circular No. A-94: Guidelines and Discount Rates for Benefit-Cost Analysis of Federal Programs (The White House)](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/CircularA-94.pdf) - The official federal standard establishing analytical procedures, net present value calculations, discount rates, and shadow pricing for evaluating public programs.
- [The Green Book: Central Government Guidance on Appraisal and Evaluation (HM Treasury)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-green-book-appraisal-and-evaluation-in-central-government/the-green-book-2020) - The UK government’s benchmark handbook on social cost-benefit analysis, option generation, distributional impact assessment, and risk adjustment.
- [Cost-Benefit Analysis Guidance Note (Australian Government Office of Impact Analysis)](https://oia.pmc.gov.au/resources/guidance/cost-benefit-analysis) - A practical, step-by-step regulatory appraisal guide providing standard valuation techniques, sensitivity tests, and decision criteria.
- [Regional Input-Output Modeling System (RIMS II) User's Guide (U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis)](https://www.bea.gov/resources/methodologies/RIMSII-user-guide) - Technical guidance detailing how regional input-output multipliers are applied to evaluate the indirect economic effects of investments and policy interventions.
